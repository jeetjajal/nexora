# NEXORA — BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATION

## 1. Brand Core
- **Brand Name**: NEXORA
- **Tagline**: Quick Commerce, Simplified. (10-Minute Rapid Delivery)
- **Visual Personality**: Fast, Fresh, Trustworthy, Modern, Tech-Enabled

## 3. Logo Architecture & Variations

1. **Primary Horizontal Logo**: Monogram icon + bold geometric `NEXORA` wordmark + `10-MIN QUICK COMMERCE` subtitle.
2. **App Icon**: Squircle container with Leaf Green gradient background, geometric speed diagonal in Zesty Lime, and white trust pillars (`frontend/public/assets/brand/app-icon.svg`).
3. **Standalone Monogram Symbol**: Letter 'N' geometry with speed bevel and forward arrow (`frontend/public/assets/brand/icon-symbol.svg`).
4. **Favicon**: Optimized 32x32 vector squircle for browsers (`frontend/public/favicon.svg`).
5. **Approved Backgrounds**: Fresh Cream (`#F7F8F2`), White (`#FFFFFF`), or Leaf Green (`#176B45`).

## 4. Unified Ecosystem Modules Under One URL

1. **Customer Web Storefront**:
   - 10-Minute Rapid Delivery Address Bar
   - Instant Search & 31 Blinkit-Style Categories
   - Interactive Product Cards with Add/Remove counter
   - Slide-out Cart Drawer with Coupon `NEXORA50`
   - Razorpay / COD Checkout Modal
   - Real-time Order Tracking with Live Progress steps & Delivery OTP
   - Nexora AI Shopping Assistant Chatbot
2. **Delivery Partner Mobile Web**:
   - Duty Toggle (Online/Offline)
   - Active Order Pickup from Dark Store & Customer Drop
   - 6-Digit Doorstep Customer OTP verification
   - Daily & Weekly Earnings Tracker
3. **Operations Control Tower**:
   - Gross Order Value & Dispatch Radar
   - Real-time Order Stream Table with SLA tracking
   - Dark Store Hubs & Fleet Performance
4. **Dark Store Operations (StoreOps)**:
   - Aisle Zone Picking Queue
   - Barcode Scanner Simulation & Bag Sealing
